# 0001: Use case: GDPR right-of-access desk

**Decision.** Build a desk that answers GDPR Article 15 access requests for a fictional online shop.

**Why.**
- It is a real, recurring obligation for every company that holds personal data, and small companies handle it by
  hand.
- Its quality questions have checkable answers: right person, all records, every third party hidden, correct
  deadline. That allows exact evals rather than opinions.
- Only one step, releasing the reply, can cause harm. That makes a clean human gate.

**Given up.** Other data-subject rights (erasure, rectification, portability) are detected and routed to a person
rather than automated.
